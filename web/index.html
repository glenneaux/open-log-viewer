TS Log Viewer
v1.10.0
A viewer for Haltech, Ecutek (ProECU), MaxxECU, Link (G4X/G4+/G5), MoTeC i2 Pro (.ld), SuperFlow dyno (.ptd / .ptda) and EMtron (.elf) logs. Auto-detects the log format and switches to a workspace tailored to it, with separate page tabs, favorites, math channels, and data grid per ECU type. Built by TuneSmith.
